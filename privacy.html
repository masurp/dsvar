What this site knows about you

Privacy

This is a static teaching site. There is no account to make, no form to fill in, and nothing here that is trying to learn who you are. Apart from the request for the page itself, one thing leaves your browser, and this page says what it is.

THE SHORT VERSION

The site has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies at all. It has no comments, logins or forms, and nothing you do on these pages is reported back to anyone who teaches this course.

The one exception: the fonts are loaded from Google, so Google sees that a browser at your address asked for them. The details are below.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR BROWSER

Two requests: one to Google for the fonts, and the request for the page itself that every website receives.

The fonts, from Google. Every page asks fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com for Space Grotesk and Newsreader. To answer, Google receives your IP address, your browser and operating system version, and the address of the page you are reading. This is how most of the web loads fonts and it is still a transfer of data to a third party, in the United States, governed by Google’s privacy policy rather than by anything here. It can be removed by serving the font files from this site instead, which is a change to how the site is built and not to anything you can do from your end.

The page itself, from the web server. The site is published with GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub’s logs the requests it answers, including your IP address and which page you asked for. That is GitHub’s processing under their privacy statement, and nobody teaching this course can read those logs or has asked for them.

Nothing else on any page makes an outside request. The charts, the interactive explainers, the gallery exhibits, the datasets and the search index are all files served from this site.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

No cookies are set, first or third party. You can confirm it in your browser’s storage inspector.

Three small values may be written to this site’s local storage, which is a private notepad your browser keeps per website. They exist so the site remembers how you like to read it:

Value What it is for When it appears
quarto-color-scheme whether you chose the light or the dark version when you use the toggle in the navigation bar
quarto-reader-mode whether you collapsed the side margins when you use reader mode
quarto-persistent-tabsets-data which tab of a tabbed block you last opened on any page load

These stay in your browser. They are never transmitted anywhere, they are not readable by this site’s authors or by anyone else, and clearing your browser data deletes them. They are set by Quarto, the publishing system this site is built with, and not by anything written for this course.

THINGS THAT ARE NOT THIS SITE

This page covers these pages. Three things you will use for this course are somewhere else and have their own terms.

  • Canvas is where the course runs: enrolment, submissions, grades and the route to a teacher. It belongs to Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and is covered by the university’s privacy statement, not by this one.
  • Links that leave the site (to a paper, a dataset’s home, a newspaper’s chart) take you to someone else’s server on their terms. The credits page lists where the borrowed material comes from.
  • Downloading the datasets is an ordinary file download from this site. What you then do with the data, especially in your portfolio, is your responsibility, and the course’s rules on that are on the assessment page.

CHECKING THIS YOURSELF

This course spends eight weeks arguing that a claim should be checkable by the person receiving it, so it would be poor form to ask you to take this page on trust. Open your browser’s developer tools, load any page here with the network tab recording, and read the list of requests. You should see this site’s own files and two requests to Google’s font servers. If you ever see something else, that is a mistake and worth reporting.

Questions, or something that looks wrong: contact the course coordinator through Canvas. There is no address printed here on purpose, because an address on a public page is harvested within days.
